Use case: ui-mockup. A complete high fidelity native Android app screen, tall portrait 9:20, edge to edge without phone bezel or presentation background. It is a design concept for a Nikon NCP preset browser, not a screenshot of a released product. Users want to see sample photos, download to their phone, and later import a preset to a camera via USB.

Direction C: familiar Material-inspired Android hierarchy. Stable ivory-white surfaces and dark warm charcoal text, a rich muted yellow selected-tab field and yellow primary controls with dark text, using Chinese system sans-serif and recognizable native controls. No imitation Nikon corporate logo, no dark experimental dashboard, no oversized hero headline. The signature is a calm image catalog with a very clear persistent local-file entry.

Android status bar at top. Compact app title “预设库”, a small help control, tabs “在线预设” and “我的预设” with online active, a quiet device status “未连接相机”. Main content is a clean vertical photo catalog, with one large featured photograph followed by two compact horizontal image-and-text rows, enough content to prove the hierarchy. Synthetic entries “晴日胶片” (sunlit coastal street), “青山淡彩” (green mountain river), “夜色微光” (city evening). The first two download states are “未下载” and “已下载”, clearly written beneath names. Bottom fixed tonal action “从手机添加 NCP” sits above the Android gesture region, not covering the list. Photos have “概念样片” and a discreet final note says “界面设计示意 · 非预设实拍”. No search, account, shopping, favorites, ratings, imaginary compatibility certificates or decorative metrics. Polish spacing, alignment and accessible button hierarchy. All visible Chinese must be exact, clean and legible.
